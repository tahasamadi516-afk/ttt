# Channel character

Reference art: `character-sheet.png` (same folder).

## Look (keep these consistent in every shot)
- Man, late 20s to 30s, lean build, sharp jaw, strong brows, olive/tan skin
- Dark curly hair, messy, falls over the forehead
- Oversized black t-shirt (dropped shoulders, elbow-length sleeves)
- Loose black cuffed pants (jogger cut)
- Brown leather strap sandals
- Art style: semi-realistic comic/anime line art, warm dark palette, reddish rim
  light on the outlines, flat charcoal-grey background

## Poses on the sheet
- Full body, front: standing, hands in pockets
- Full body, back: same stance
- Sitting on the ground, arm resting on knee
- Crouching, chin on hand (bored / thinking)
- Pointing at the viewer

## Expressions on the sheet
- Neutral, intense stare
- Smug half-smile, chin up
- Laughing, eyes closed
- Explaining, hand raised, mid-sentence
- Looking down, defeated / thinking
- Side glance, skeptical

## How he is used in videos
A mix, chosen per topic:
- Simple character animation (pose and expression swaps, light movement)
- Motion graphics (text, maps, charts, diagrams around him)
- Documentary sections (footage, photos, narration)
