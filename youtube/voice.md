# Voice profile

Built from the video 01 script and the creator's answers.

## Who it's for
Broad: English speakers, mainly Americans, mainly ages 15 to 35.
Viewer to write for: someone in that range with big goals who has been losing
months to dating apps and situationships, and half-knows he's wasting his time.

## How he talks
- Storytime, first person, like talking to a friend. Starts mid-moment
  ("this morning I'm making my breakfast...").
- Makes fun of himself first ("Genius over here", "Stupid idiot") before he says
  anything to the viewer. He never preaches from above: "I'm not saying I'm better than her."
- Short punchy lines, rhetorical questions answered right away ("Was it? No. Of course not.")
- Acts out other people's inner voice in quotes ("Maybe I'm good enough!")
- Throws in random real-time asides ("my neighbor is looking at me from his window")
- Everyday metaphors that hit hard: the cat that leaves, the knife, gold being just a metal
- Anime references (Gojo's Infinity, Mahoraga)
- Swears freely (fuck, shit) for emphasis
- Comedy sitting on a sincere core. He ends warm ("Don't waste it, man. Love you all equally")
- Faith comes up lightly and without pushing ("I believe that's from God. Whatever you think it's from, up to you.")

## Words he uses
bro, man, stupid, idiot, crazy, "use your mind", "for real", "anyway(s)",
"wait. Wait a minute.", "relax", "find a way"

## Words he never uses
- "son of a bitch"
- Swearing in general, unless he chooses it for emphasis. When writing for him,
  don't add swear words. Keep only the ones he put in himself.

## Claims he won't make
Not stated yet. Default from the script: speak from his own experience and
opinion, and never invent stats or studies.

## Format
- Length: about 9 minutes
- Cadence: as often as videos are ready (daily is the goal). Publish at 6 AM.
- Style: personal storytime essay that ends on a wider point about life. Visuals: the animated character, motion graphics, and
documentary sections.

## Video style (creator's direction)
- **Tone of the visuals:** a guy talking about life, making fun of himself and having
  fun with the audience. It's comedy on top of something real, never a lecture.
- **The character's face must match the line.** Every beat gets the right reaction
  (smug, cringe, disbelief, deadpan, laughing, sincere). Don't reuse a neutral pose
  just because it's there.
- **Music: no instrumental music.** Use a cappella only (voices, hums, choir), and
  never anything corny. Reference mood: the a cappella versions of "Bring Me to
  Life", "In and Out of Love" and "Funeral of Hearts". Dark, emotional, dramatic
  2000s gothic/trance vocal feel, used where it fits the moment.
  Those songs themselves are copyrighted. Use original or licensed a cappella
  in that style, so videos don't get Content ID claims.
