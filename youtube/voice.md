# Voice profile

Built from the video 01 script, the creator's answers, and an earlier session's profile
(from their own writing, "The Friend Who Won't Lie To You"; merged from branch ccr-4c1eca1d-8a0tii).

## The channel
Thinking out loud. He sits down with an idea and talks it through, step by step, until he
lands somewhere real. Topics: what's going on in his head, around him, in society and
culture. Why do people react like this? Why am I reacting like this? Philosophical, but
grounded in real moments from his own life.

## Who it's for
Broad: English speakers, mainly Americans, mainly ages 15 to 35.
Viewer to write for: someone in that range with big goals who has been losing
months to dating apps and situationships, and half-knows he's wasting his time.
He talks differently to each age:
- **Kids:** motivate. Make them ambitious, make them feel the world is okay.
- **Teens:** start warning them a little.
- **Around 21 (his age):** brutally honest, like talking to himself.
- **Older people:** willing to call them out and critique how they see things.

## How he talks
- Storytime, first person, like talking to a friend. Starts mid-moment
  ("this morning I'm making my breakfast...").
- Makes fun of himself first ("Genius over here", "Stupid idiot") before he says
  anything to the viewer. He never preaches from above: "I'm not saying I'm better than her."
- Short punchy lines, rhetorical questions answered right away ("Was it? No. Of course not.")
- Acts out other people's inner voice in quotes ("Maybe I'm good enough!")
- Throws in random real-time asides ("my neighbor is looking at me from his window")
- Everyday metaphors that hit hard: the cat that leaves, the knife, gold being just a metal
- Anime references (Gojo's Infinity, Mahoraga)
- Swears freely (fuck, shit) for emphasis
- Comedy sitting on a sincere core. He ends warm ("Don't waste it, man. Love you all equally")
- The friend who won't lie to you: honest to the point of rude, but it comes from caring.
- Calls out your excuse before you make it. Ends on a push to act, not a soft summary.
- Cuts the fluff: "That's it." "Done."
- Faith comes up lightly and without pushing ("I believe that's from God. Whatever you think it's from, up to you.")

## Words he uses
bro, man, stupid, idiot, crazy, "use your mind", "for real", "anyway(s)",
"wait. Wait a minute.", "relax", "find a way", deserve, work for it, get up, do it,
step by step, don't overcomplicate it, honestly, not gonna lie, trust me,
you know what I'm saying, the struggle, act like it

## Words he never uses
- "son of a bitch"
- Swearing in general, unless he chooses it for emphasis. When writing for him,
  don't add swear words. Keep only the ones he put in himself.

## Rule for scripts
He gives raw ideas in his own words (spoken-style; English is his second language).
- Keep his exact words, ideas and tone. Don't invent new ideas, facts, stats or advice.
- Reframe into a video: strongest line first as the hook, tight order, a landing at the end.
- Fix grammar quietly, but keep it sounding like him. No fancy words he wouldn't say.

## Claims he won't make
Not stated yet. Default from the script: speak from his own experience and
opinion, and never invent stats or studies.

## Voice (audio)
**Channel voice: "E"**, a designed voice (male, early 20s, General American, clear medium-deep,
natural and grounded, like thinking out loud to a friend). Qwen3-TTS, Apache 2.0, free for
commercial use. Details: `youtube/voice-clone/voice-e.md`. Narrate with `narrate_qwen.py`.
Scripts must be written FOR this voice: short punchy sentences that end hard, no filler, no
acted-out voices (put those on screen instead). Video 01 v1 vs v3 shows the difference.
Swearing: f-words bleeped; "shit" becomes "crap", "hell" becomes "heck".
Older options kept as fallbacks: Kokoro am_puck (`narrate_kokoro.py`), his own clone (`narrate.py`).

## Format
- Faceless: voiceover plus the animated character, motion graphics and documentary visuals.
- Long form (~9 min), cut into ~9 one-minute shorts. Each short stands alone: one idea, one landing.
- Posted to YouTube and Instagram.
- Length: about 9 minutes
- Cadence: as often as videos are ready (daily is the goal). Publish at 6 AM.
- Style: personal storytime essay that ends on a wider point about life. Visuals: the animated character, motion graphics, and
documentary sections.

## Video style (creator's direction)
- **Tone of the visuals:** a guy talking about life, making fun of himself and having
  fun with the audience. It's comedy on top of something real, never a lecture.
- **The character's face must match the line.** Every beat gets the right reaction
  (smug, cringe, disbelief, deadpan, laughing, sincere). Don't reuse a neutral pose
  just because it's there.
- **Music: no instrumental music.** Use a cappella only (voices, hums, choir), and
  never anything corny. Reference mood: the a cappella versions of "Bring Me to
  Life", "In and Out of Love" and "Funeral of Hearts". Dark, emotional, dramatic
  2000s gothic/trance vocal feel, used where it fits the moment.
  Those songs themselves are copyrighted. Use original or licensed a cappella
  in that style, so videos don't get Content ID claims.
- **The epic-music joke:** dramatic a cappella never plays over a normal room like the
  kitchen. When the choir comes in, he's suddenly somewhere epic: a medieval castle,
  a candlelit throne room, a cathedral, heaven with light beams. The lighting and
  environment match the music, played completely straight. When the music cuts, hard
  cut back to his normal room. The contrast is the joke.
